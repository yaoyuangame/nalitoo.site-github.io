Four things we will not do

Read this bit
01

We will not sell your data

We do not sell personal information to brokers. What we collect is described in plain language in our privacy policy.

02

We will not gate progress behind payment

Purchases are cosmetic or convenience only. You can reach the end of our games without spending anything.

03

We will not punish you for leaving

No streaks that decay, no timers that expire, no notifications nagging you back. Your save file waits patiently.

04

We will not ship a known crash

Stability fixes go out before new content. A crashing game is not a game, it is a bug with artwork.

Talk to a human

Publishers, partners, players and press are all welcome. Bug reports get read by the people who wrote the code. Write to the studio inbox and you will hear back.
